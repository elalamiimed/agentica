     会议详情
      Event Highlights
2026年7月19日至21日，第二届鹏海模拟联合国大会在深圳北理莫斯科大学（SMBU）成功举办。
大会设四个会场，议题涵盖1923鲁尔危机、明金辽东之战及COP30气候大会等，各校优秀代表齐
聚一堂，展开思辨与交锋。


From July 19–21, 2026, the 2nd Penghai Model United Nations Conference was successfully
held at Shenzhen MSU-BIT University (SMBU), featuring four committees on topics ranging
from the 1923 Ruhr Crisis to COP30.




与鹏海模联2026组委会合作，哈工大（深圳）国际青年俱乐部组织了本校15名国际学生代表参
会。在为期三天的谈判与辩论中，代表们准备充分、立场明确、沟通能力突出，充分展现了哈工大
深圳校区国际学生的精神风貌。


In partnerships with the Penghai MUN 2026, HSIYC coordinated our school’s delegation of 15
international students. Over three days of negotiation and debate, the delegates
demonstrated thorough preparation, clear positions, and outstanding communication skills,
proudly showcasing the spirit of HITSZ’s international community.

本届鹏海模联，是一次思想的交融，也是一段成长的旅程。愿这份荣誉成为新的起点，激励更多国
际学子勇敢发声、连接世界。


This year's Penghai MUN was both a clash of ideas and a journey of growth. May this honor be
a new beginning that inspires more international students to speak up and connect with the
world.
