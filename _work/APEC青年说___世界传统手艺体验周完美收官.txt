                                     第一站 · 中国 | China
                                   折剪之间：中国纸艺传统
                              The Art of Folding and Cutting




2026年5月21日，APEC文化绘卷的第一站，从剪纸艺术的灵动之美中徐徐展开。
同学们亲手体验了这项传承千年的非物质文化遗产——一把剪刀，一张红纸，便能剪出花鸟鱼
虫，裁出人间百态。每一道剪痕都藏着温暖的祝福，每一幅作品都是独一无二的故事。传统与现
代在指尖悄然交汇，大家由衷地感受到中华文化跨越时空的独特魅力。


On May 21st, our cultural journey began with one of China's most iconic art forms— paper
cutting.
Members picked up scissors and red paper, and within moments, flowers, birds, and intricate
patterns came to life in their hands. This intangible cultural heritage, passed down for over a
thousand years, carries blessings and endless creativity. For many, it was their first time
experiencing this ancient craft and the smiles in the room said it all.

                               第二站 · 孟加拉国 | Bangladesh
                                  传统之彩：孟加拉民俗工艺
                          Colors of Tradition: Bengali Folk Crafts




5月22日，校园里迎来了孟加拉国最鲜艳的色彩和最欢快的节奏。
同学们亲手制作了三件具有代表性的传统手工艺品：象征孟加拉新年的拜沙基面具、图案精美的诺
克希扇子，还有简易打击乐器达格杜吉。工作坊里笑声不断，每个人都沉浸在浓浓的孟加拉乡村风
情里。这一晚，来自不同国家的同学们一起感受到了孟加拉文化那份真实又鲜活的魅力。


On May 22nd, Bangladesh painted our campus with its most vibrant colors and rhythms.
Members handcrafted three traditional items: Baisakhi Masks used during the Bengali New Year,
Nokshi Fans decorated with intricate local patterns, and Dugdugis — simple yet joyful percussion
instruments. The workshop was filled with laughter, creativity, and genuine cultural exchange. By
the end of the evening, everyone had taken home not just a handmade craft, but a piece of
Bangladesh in their hearts.
              特别嘉宾 · Special Guest
人文艺术学系教授黄红宇老师亲临现场，与同学们共同体验中国与孟加拉国的文化活动，黄老师的
到来为活动增添了别样的温暖与意义。她表示，这样富有创意的文化交流活动，正是让哈工大深圳
校区走向真正国际化的重要一步，期待未来有更多精彩的文化活动在这里绽放。

initiative as a creative and joyful way to foster cultural sharing on campus, and expressed her hope
to see more such events in the future — events that make our campus truly international, one
culture at a time.

                                  第三站 · 摩洛哥 | Morocco
                                 麦地那寻踪：摩洛哥文化探秘
                         The Medina Quest: A Moroccan Journey
5月23日，我们开启了一场充满神秘色彩的摩洛哥之旅。这次活动的主题是《麦地那寻宝》，我们带
着同学们穿越时空，走进摩洛哥古老的老城区。
现场准备了六种代代相传的传统游戏，包括拉玛丽、比伊弹珠、拉斯蒂克跳绳，还有悠闲的薄荷茶
文化等。每一种游戏背后，都藏着一个摩洛哥人世代流传的故事。大家在欢声笑语中互动探索，距
离不知不觉拉近了，友谊也悄悄生了根。


On May 23rd, we stepped into the ancient streets of Morocco's medina. The theme — The Medina
Quest — brought six traditional games to life, each passed down through generations.
From the strategy of La Marie to the skill of Lbiyy marbles, from the energy of Lastik jump rope to
the calm of sharing mint tea, every game told a story of Moroccan life. Members competed,
laughed, and connected in ways that only play can bring — proving that culture needs no
translation.

                              第四站 · 印度尼西亚 | Indonesia
                               色彩与传承：印尼文化工作坊
                       Warna & Warisan: Indonesian Workshop




5月24日，印度尼西亚的色彩与创意，让我们同时享受到视觉和手作的双重乐趣。
工作坊把传统蜡染艺术和童年游戏融合在一起，同学们亲手制作了充满印尼风情的蜡染手提袋，
每个人画出的图案都是独一无二的。接着，大家又体验了印尼经典传统游戏bola bekel——道具
虽然简单，笑声却格外纯粹，让所有人都找回了童年的感觉。这一晚，印尼文化用最朴实的方式
告诉我们：艺术，原来可以是最简单的快乐。


On May 24th, Indonesia brought us a feast of color, creativity, and joy.
The workshop blended two beautiful things: the ancient art of batik and the innocent fun of
childhood games. Members designed their own batik-inspired tote bags, each one a unique
work of art. Then came bola bekel — a classic Indonesian game that had everyone laughing
and competing with full hearts. It was a reminder that the most beautiful cultural experiences
are the ones that make you feel like a child again.

      第五站 · 吉尔吉斯斯坦 | Kyrgyzstan
      探索吉尔吉斯斯坦：游牧文化与传统
     Discover Kyrgyzstan: Nomad Culture
5月25日，来自中亚草原的游牧文化，为文化绘卷带来了不一样的艺术气息。吉尔吉斯斯坦——这片
被天山环抱的神秘土地，用传统的图案和毡毯设计，向我们讲述着游牧民族的智慧与信仰。
在活动过程中，同学们了解了那些古老符号背后的深意，并亲手创作出属于自己的吉尔吉斯风格艺
术品。那一刻，每个人都仿佛成了中亚草原上自由游牧的旅人。


On May 25th, the vast steppes of Central Asia arrived at our campus. Kyrgyzstan, a land of
mountains and nomads, shared its ancient soul through traditional patterns and felt designs.
Members learned the sacred meanings behind each symbol — patterns that have guided nomadic
life for centuries. Then, guided by their own imagination, they created unique pieces of Kyrgyz-
inspired artwork. It was a journey to the heart of a culture that lives in harmony with nature and the
open sky.

           第六站 · 俄罗斯 | Russia
       科科什尼克传说：俄罗斯手工艺
     Tales of Kokoshnik: Crafts of Russia
5月27日，世界传统手艺体验周活动在俄罗斯的优雅与精致中画上了圆满的句号。
本次工作坊的主题是科科什尼克——这顶象征女性气质、艺术与庇护的传奇头饰，承载着俄罗斯
数百年的历史与文化记忆。同学们一起探索了它的古老起源、神圣寓意，以及精美的金线刺绣技
艺，真切感受到这项至今仍在启发现代时尚的传奇文化遗产的无穷魅力。旅程在这里画上了句
点，但那份对俄罗斯文化的深深感动，会一直留在每个人心里。


On May 27th, our cultural journey reached its most elegant finale — Russia.
The workshop centered on the kokoshnik, a legendary headdress that has symbolized Russian
womanhood, artistry, and protection for centuries. Members uncovered its ancient origins,
sacred meanings, and the breathtaking craft of gold embroidery that still influences modern
fashion today. As the final stop of our seven-day journey, Russia left us with a sense of awe — a
reminder that culture, like art, is timeless, boundless, and deeply human.
