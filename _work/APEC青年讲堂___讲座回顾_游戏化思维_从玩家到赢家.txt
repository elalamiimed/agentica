                              游戏，究竟是什么？
                              WHAT IS A GAME?

                    APEC Youth Lecture | Gamification



讲座以一个看似简单的问题开场：强制性的团建活动还可以视为游戏吗？多邻国是
游戏吗？蚂蚁森林呢？


陈教授通过日常生活中的例子，引导大家重新思考“游戏”与“游戏化”的界限——游戏
必须是自愿参与的、以挑战为驱动的、本身具有意义的，并且建立在规则体系之
上。


The lecture opened with a deceptively simple question: is a mandatory team-
building activity still a game? Is Duolingo a game? What about Ant Forest?


Through everyday examples, Prof. Chen guided the audience to redefine the
boundary between "game" and "gamification" — a game must be voluntary,
obstacle-driven, purposeful in itself, and built on a system of rules.
                         科学理论与八角分析法
                  THE SCIENCE & THE FRAMEWORK

                  APEC Youth Lecture | Gamification



讲座围绕三个核心理论展开：心流理论、外在动机与内在动机。通过从巴甫洛夫实
验到自我决定理论的层层推进，陈教授揭示了某些体验让人沉浸其中的深层原因。


在此基础上，陈教授引入了八角行为分析法——驱动人类行为的八大核心动力：
使命感 · 成就感
创造力 · 拥有感
社交影响 · 稀缺性
未知性 · 损失规避



The lecture covered three core theories:
Flow State, Extrinsic and Intrinsic Motivation. From Pavlov’s dogs to Self-
Determination Theory, Prof. Chen revealed why certain experiences keep us
hooked.


Building on this, she introduced the Octalysis Framework — eight core drives
behind human motivation:
Epic Meaning · Accomplishment
Creativity · Ownership
Social Influence · Scarcity
Unpredictability · Loss & Avoidance




                         学以致用，现场闯关！
                     GAMIFICATION IN ACTION

                 APEC Youth Lecture | Gamification




讲座最后，一场游戏化互动测验把现场气氛推向了高潮。同学们现场答题，检验自
己对讲座内容的理解，得分最高的三位同学获得了社团专属纪念品，陈教授也收到
了同款礼物。

The session wrapped up with a live gamified quiz that tested the key takeaways
from the lecture. Students answered concept-based questions in real time - the
top three scorers each received an exclusive piece of club merchandise, and
Prof. Yang Chen was also presented with one.
                           游戏从来不只是游戏
                     Games Were Never Just Games

                  APEC Youth Lecture | Gamification




这场讲座留给我们的，不止是一套理论框架，更是一个值得反复琢磨的问题：既然
世界上最好的游戏都在想方设法让我们沉浸其中——那我们为什么不用同样的方
式，来设计自己的生活呢？

This lecture left us with more than a framework. It left us with a question worth
asking: if the world’s best games are engineered to keep us engaged — what
stops us from designing our own lives the same way?
