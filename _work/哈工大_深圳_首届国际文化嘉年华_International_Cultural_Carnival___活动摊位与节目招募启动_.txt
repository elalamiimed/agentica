1.招募要求 / Requirement
欢迎各国学生展示本国文化、传统手工艺、美食、服饰等，打造多元文化互动体验区！
我们会组织摊位审核，因场地有限，会根据具体报名情况进行调整，数量有限先到先得！
We invite students from all countries to showcase their unique national cultures! You
are encouraged to present traditional crafts, cuisine, costumes, and more at our vibrant
multicultural interactive experience area.
Please note that due to high demand and limited booth space, applications will be
subject to review and arrangements are subject to adjustment. Registrations are
accepted on first-come, first-served basis.

1.招募要求 / Requirement
内容积极向上，体现文化特色或创新融合；
All performances should convey a positive message, demonstrating distinct cultural
identity or innovative fusion.
节目时长适中，以5分钟内为佳；
The duration of each performance should be preferably within 5 minutes.
个人或团队形式报名均可；
Sign up as an individual or as a team.


2.节目类型 / Performance Type
歌曲演唱（独唱、合唱等）
Singing (solo, chorus…)
乐队表演（摇滚、民谣等）
Band performances (pop, folk..)
舞蹈（民族舞、街舞、现代舞等）
Dance (traditional, street, modern…)
其他才艺（乐器、魔术等）
Other talents (instrumental, magic show..)


3.报名方式 / How to Sign Up
请先填写在线申请表格，并加入微信群以了解更多活动信息。我们会根据报名情况陆续安排
面试，请大家关注后续通知。
Please complete the online application form and join the WeChat group below for
further details. Interviews will be scheduled, and all applicants are advised to stay tuned
for further updates.
