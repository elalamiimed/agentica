APEC青年讲堂 | 讲座预告：游戏化思维——从玩家到赢家
Original   国际教育中心 哈工大深圳校区国际教育中心 Jun 11, 2026, 3:02 PM 广东
  为什么游戏让人上瘾，而学习却让人疲惫？怎么才能让枯燥的任务变得有意
  思？如何用设计激发真正的行为改变？答案就是“游戏化”——把游戏里的设计
  用到非游戏的场景中，激发大家的参与感、动力和习惯养成。


  在全球化时代，想抓住别人的注意力越来越难，而游戏化提供了一套有趣且有
  效的方法：它让人“愿意”做原本枯燥的事。本次讲座，未来设计学院副教授陈
  阳博士将带你完成一次认知升级——从被动的“玩家”，成为设计规则、驱动行
  为的“赢家”。


  Why are games so addictive while learning feels exhausting? How can we
  make dull tasks engaging? The answer lies in gamification—using game
  elements in non-game contexts to drive user engagement, motivation,
  and habits.


  Capturing attention is harder than ever in today's globalised world.
  Gamification offers a fun and effective way to make people willing to do
  what once felt tedious. In this lecture, Dr. Yang Chen will guide you
  through a cognitive upgrade — from being a passive player to becoming
  a winner who designs rules and drives behavior.




                            讲座简介
                        Why This Lecture?

                   Gamification Design



陈阳博士将带领大家直击问题核心：游戏化到底为什么有用？如何才能设计出真正
改变行为的游戏化方案？


我们会带你完成一次思路上的大升级：从只对“积分、徽章”有模糊印象，到掌握“动
机驱动设计”的核心逻辑——在过程中你不仅体验到游戏化的乐趣，还学会设计规
则、驱动参与、创造真实影响。



Dr. Chen will guide us straight to the core: Why does gamification work? How
do we design gamified solutions that truly change behavior?


We will guide you through a clear methodological upgrade: from a vague
impression of "points and badges" to mastering the core logic of
motivation-driven design - not just experiencing the fun of gamification, but
learning to design rules, drive engagement, and create real impact.
                                嘉宾简介
                                Why Her?

                    Gamification Design



陈阳博士，哈尔滨工业大学（深圳）未来设计学院副教授。她曾主持多项国家社科
基金与广东省社科基金项目，研究成果发表于《国际人机交互期刊》（SCI索引）及
多个顶级国际会议。她的研究聚焦于游戏化如何驱动学生的参与度、学习动机与行
为改变。


陈博士不仅学术成果扎实，更以接地气、学生为本的教学风格深受欢迎。她近期对
蚂蚁森林（中国著名环保游戏化应用）的研究，揭示了微小设计选择如何带来真实
的行为改变。她的座右铭“Have fun in gamification”完美诠释了她的理念：让学
习与设计变得有趣、有意义。


Dr. Yang Chen is an Associate Professor at the Future Design School, HITSZ. She
has received multiple research grants from the Chinese National Social Science
Foundation and Guangdong Provincial Social Science Foundation.


Her publications appear in leading journals such as the International Journal of
Human-Computer Interaction (SCI indexed) and top conferences. Her research
focuses on how gamification drives student engagement, learning motivation,
and behavior change.


Beyond her research, Dr. Chen is known for her down‑to‑earth,
student‑centered approach. Her recent work on Ant Forest (China’s famous
pro‑environmental gamification app) reveals how small design choices can
drive real behavioral change.
"Have fun in gamification" — her personal motto says it all.
