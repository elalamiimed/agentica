国际青年俱乐部 | APEC智慧校园创新系列竞赛
Original   国际教育中心 哈工大深圳校区国际教育中心 Jun 16, 2026, 11:29 AM 广东




           近日，哈工大（深圳）国际青年俱乐部（HSIYC）正式推出“APEC智慧校园创新系
           列”——三场独立竞赛，灵感来自2026年11月深圳承办APEC峰会。


           每场竞赛对应亚太共同体的一个核心理念：创新（面向未来的能力）、合作（一起
           创造解决方案）和开放（拥抱文化多样性）。三场竞赛共同将我们的校园转化为
           APEC愿景的实践缩影。


           HITSZ International Youth Club (HSIYC) proudly launches the APEC Smart
           Campus Innovation Series – three independent competitions inspired by
           Shenzhen hosting the APEC Summit in November 2026.
Each competition reflects a core value of the Asia-Pacific community:
Innovation (future-ready skills), Cooperation (co-creating solutions together)
and Openness (embracing cultural diversity). Together, they turn our campus
into a living example for APEC’s vision.

                           INNOVATION

 随着APEC各经济体竞相迈入AI驱动的新阶段，快速解决复杂问题的能力变得前所
 未有的重要。


 本系列竞赛的灵感源自传奇的MIT积分大赛。其中第一场竞赛集中体现APEC的创
 新精神——夯实数学基础，而这些基础正是机器学习、数据科学以及整个亚太地
 区前沿研究的根基。


 As APEC economies race toward an AI‑driven future, the ability to solve
 complex problems quickly has never been more essential.


 Inspired by the legendary MIT Integration Bee. This competition embodies
 the INNOVATION spirit of APEC – sharpening the mathematical foundations
 that power machine learning, data science, and cutting‑edge research across
 the Asia‑Pacific.
Why should you join?
为什么你要参加？

                                  赛制与规则
                            Format & Rules

    单败淘汰制（1对1），两人同时解题
    每场比赛3道积分题（预赛轮）
    每场比赛胜者=解题正确数更多的一方（若双方均解对，则用时更短者胜；
    BO3赛制）
    平局→加赛一题，先答对者获胜
    禁止使用计算器或任何电子设备
    由裁判判定谁先作答（根据举手或喊答顺序）
    裁判的判定为最终结果



    Single‑elimination bracket (1v1), both participants solve
    simultaneously
    Each match: 3 integrals (Preliminary Round)
    Winner of the match = participant who solved more problems (or
    faster if both solved correctly; BO3)
    Tie → sudden‑death integrals: first correct answer wins the match
    No calculators or electronic devices
    Judge decides who answered first (based on hand raise/ call)
    Judges' decisions are final
适合人群：微积分高手、极限运算控、所有热爱脑力挑战的同学。

Perfect for Calculus masters, speed solvers, and anyone who loves a mental
adrenaline rush.

                           COOPERATION

随 着 今 年 11 月 APEC 峰 会 落 地 深 圳 ， 合 作 浪 潮 正 重 塑 我 们 的 学 习 、 工 作 与 生 活
——而游戏化正是推动这一变化最有力的工具之一。本次竞赛邀请校区全体学
生，运用积分、徽章、排行榜、连续打卡、挑战任务、奖励等机制，解决一个真
实的校园问题，体现APEC的“合作”精神。鼓励大家使用AI工具。


With APEC arriving in Shenzhen this November, cooperation across the Asia-
Pacific is reshaping how we learn, work, and live — and gamification is one
of the most powerful tools driving that shift. This competition invites all
HITSZ students to design a gamified system — using mechanics like points,
badges, leaderboards, streaks, challenges, and rewards — to solve a real
campus problem and embody the COOPERATION spirit of APEC. Use of AI
tools is highly encouraged.

DEMO VIDEO STRUCTURE
演示视频结构
视频须按以下顺序呈现：
1. 参赛者介绍—静态标题页（至少3秒），包含姓名、学号、专业及年级、手机
  号
2. 系统架构—仅展示图表，不得使用大段文字或配音讲解的幻灯片；呈现主要组
  件、连接方式及整体系统逻辑
3. 游戏化机制演示 — 核心环节，评分权重最高；机制须在屏幕上实时运行，不
  得使用屏幕文字或说明覆层
4. 附加内容（可选）—界面演示或结束语，时长不得超过视频总时长的三分之一

The video must follow this order:
1. Participant Introduction — Still title slide (min. 3 sec) with name, student
  ID, major and year, and phone number
2. System Architecture — Diagram only — no text blocks or narrated slides;
  show main components, how they connect, and overall system logic
3. Gamification Mechanics Demonstration — The core section, highest
  judging weight; mechanics must run live on screen
4. Additional Content (Optional) — Interface walkthrough or closing

JUDING CRITERIA
评审标准

本次竞赛的核心要求是：所提交的系统必须以游戏化作为其主要设计方式。游戏
化是指将游戏设计元素与机制，如积分、徽章、排行榜、连击、挑战与奖励等应
用于非游戏场景，以激励用户产生积极行为。

游戏化功能须通过实际用户操作进行演示（实时互动，而非静态展示）。例如：
  用户通过特定操作获得积分、经验值或货币
  徽章、成就或奖励被解锁
  排行榜实时更新或展示竞争排名
  升级、段位进阶或里程碑达成
  连击、挑战或任务的追踪与完成

The core requirement of this competition is that the proposed system must
use gamification as its primary approach. Gamification refers to the
application of game-design elements and mechanics — such as points,
badges, leaderboards, streaks, challenges, and rewards — in a non-game
context to motivate and engage users toward positive behaviors.

Gamified functions should be demonstrated based on actions (live
interaction, not static display). For example:
  A user earning points, XP, or currency through a specific action
  A badge, achievement, or reward being unlocked
  A leaderboard updating or displaying competitive standing
  A level-up, rank progression, or milestone being reached
  A streak, challenge, or quest being tracked or completed


 作品质量与演示清晰度比视频制作水准更重要；系统无需达到生产级别，但必须能
 够运行并清晰展示游戏化机制

  Idea quality and demo clarity matter more than production perfection; the
  system must function and clearly demonstrate gamification mechanics in
  action.

                                 OPENNESS

APEC的21个经济体各自拥有独特的传统、艺术与故事。“开放”意味着将这种多样性
融入我们的日常生活。我们不禁想问：假如我们的校园真正融合了整个APEC世界的
多元文化，它会是什么样子？


本次竞赛体现的正是APEC的开放精神。请使用AI工具（或手绘）创作3-5张图像，
将真实校园场景与任一APEC经济体的文化元素相融合，展现开放之美。


The APEC family spans 21 economies, each with unique traditions, art, and
stories. Openness means welcoming that diversity into our daily lives. So we
ask: what would our campus look like if it truly belonged to the entire APEC
world?


This competition embodies the OPENNESS spirit of APEC. Use AI (or your own
hand) to create 3–5 images that fuse real HITSZ scenes with cultural elements
from any APEC member economy. Show us the beauty of openness.
AI工具正在重新定义视觉创意的边界。本次竞赛专为校区国际学生设计，只有一个
创作规则：每张图像必须同时包含真实校园场景和一个APEC成员经济体的文化元素
——艺术风格不限。以此展现我们共同的校园，以及我们身后的多元世界。

AI tools are redefining the boundaries of visual creativity. Designed for
international students at HITSZ, this competition has one creative rule: every
image must bring together a real HITSZ campus scene and the culture of an
APEC member economy — in ANY ARTISTIC STYLE you choose. Celebrate the
campus we share and the diverse worlds we each come from.
