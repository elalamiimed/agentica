                                           1. 活动概览
                                          Event Overview




           当世界议题遇上青春热忱，一场属于中外学子的外交启蒙之旅悄然启程。2026年7月
           17日，由哈尔滨工业大学（深圳）国际青年俱乐部（HSIYC）与哈尔滨工业大学（深
           圳）国际交流协会（HSICA）联合主办的哈尔滨工业大学（深圳）第一届模拟联合国
           科普讲堂在深圳校区温暖开讲，吸引了超过40名充满热情的中外学子齐聚一堂。本次
           活动得到了团委、国际教育中心以及国际事务处的悉心指导与大力支持。


           When global issues meet youthful passion, a journey of diplomatic discovery
           quietly begins. On July 17, 2026, the HITSZ Model United Nations Boot Camp was
           warmly held on campus, jointly organized by the HITSZ International Youth Club
           (HSIYC) and the HITSZ International Communication Association (HSICA). The
           event brought together more than 40 enthusiastic Chinese and international
           students. The Boot Camp was guided and supported by the Communist Youth
           League Committee, the International Education Center, and the Office of
           International Affairs.
                             为什么举办科普讲堂？

                       Why This Boot Camp Matters?




  1. 为两场国际大会蓄力 ·
  Preparing for Two Major Conferences

模拟联合国（MUN）不仅是一场辩论游戏，更是青年学子理解世界、表达自我、锻炼
领导力的重要平台。在讲堂结束后仅两天，2026年鹏海模拟联合国大会就在深圳北理
莫斯科大学校区拉开帷幕；而一个月后，深圳国际模拟联合国大会（SZIMUN）也将
于8月19日至21日在香港中文大学（深圳）校区隆重举行。几个月前，国际青年俱乐
部先后与鹏海模联组委会以及SZIMUN的主办方香港中文大学（深圳）模联社（CUHK
MUN Club）建立起合作关系，并凭借这份合作为哈工大深圳校区学子争取到了两场大
会的注册优惠福利。为了让这份福利惠及更多中外学子，国际青年俱乐部特别联合国
际交流协会共同举办了此次科普讲堂，帮助即将奔赴大会的代表们提前打好基础。


Model United Nations is far more than a debate game — it is a vital platform for
young people to understand the world, express themselves, and cultivate
leadership. Just two days after the training concluded, the 2026 Penghai MUN
opened at the Shenzhen MSU-BIT University campus, and a month later SZIMUN
will be held from August 19 to 21 at The Chinese University of Hong Kong,
Shenzhen. A few months ago, HSIYC established partnerships with both the
Penghai MUN organizing committee and the CUHK MUN Club, the organizing
team behind SZIMUN — and through these partnerships, secured registration
discounts for HITSZ students attending both conferences. To help spread these
benefits and ensure participants were well prepared, HSIYC teamed up with HSICA
to host this Boot Camp.

  2. 培育校园模联文化 ·
  Nurturing a Campus MUN Culture

活动同时也希望进一步拓宽同学们的国际视野，提升公众演讲、外交谈判与跨文化协
作能力，并在哈工大深圳校区形成可持续发展的模联文化。就在不久前，2026年鹏海
模拟联合国大会刚刚落幕，许多参与本次科普讲堂的同学们都曾在那片舞台上挥洒汗
水、学以致用。


The event also aimed to broaden participants' international perspectives,
strengthen public speaking, negotiation, and cross-cultural teamwork skills, and
nurture a sustainable MUN culture on the HITSZ. The 2026 Penghai MUN
conference has now recently concluded, and many participants of this Boot Camp
went on to put their newly sharpened skills to the test on that very stage.

                                 环节二：模联实战模拟训练
                      SESSION 2 : Hands-On MUN Simulation Training


理论讲解之后，活动迎来了最令人期待的实战环节。新手代表们与资深模联人并肩而坐，共同体验
了人生中第一场真正意义上的委员会会议，全程由培训讲师与主席团（Dais）悉心指导。


本次模拟议题为"人工智能与教育：机遇还是挑战？——学生在使用ChatGPT等AI工具时，学校该
如何担起责任"。议题框架参考了联合国教科文组织（UNESCO）的相关指导原则，涉及人工智能
素养、学术诚信、教师培训与伦理规范等多个维度。会场上，代表们积极实践动议提出、主持核心
磋商与非正式磋商。整场辩论充分展现了中外学子在批判性思维、英语表达与协作解决问题方面的
出色能力。


Following the lectures came the most anticipated part of the day — hands-on practice.
Beginner delegates sat alongside experienced MUNers, guided by the trainers and the Dais
chairs, to experience their very first committee session.


The simulation topic was "AI in Education: Opportunity or Challenge? How Should Schools
Responsibly Use AI Tools Such as ChatGPT?" The framework drew on UNESCO guidelines,
covering AI literacy, academic integrity, teacher training, and ethics. Delegates practiced
proposing motions and navigating both moderated and unmoderated caucuses. The debate
showcased strong critical thinking, confident English expression, and genuine collaborative
problem-solving among Chinese and international students alike.

                                      更多精彩，敬请期待
                                 Looking Ahead: More to Come


      目前，活动团队正在系统整理本次培训的精华内容，倾力打造《哈工大（深圳）MUN
      入门手册》，未来将面向全校师生发布，成为大家学习模联知识的长期资源。与此同
      时，即将来临的SZIMUN大会（8月19日至21日）也向所有代表发出诚挚邀请——愿每
      一位在本次讲堂中打磨技能的青年，都能带着这份积累，再次展现属于自己的风采。


      The organizing team is currently compiling the essence of this training into the
      "HITSZ MUN Beginner's Handbook", which will soon be distributed campus-wide
      as an ongoing learning resource. Meanwhile, the upcoming SZIMUN conference
      (August 19–21, at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen) extends a
      warm invitation to all delegates — may every young person who sharpened their
      skills at this Boot Camp carry that preparation forward.
