                                 无界校园·青春共行
                                 圆满收官A Joyful Closing.




                                  HITSZ International Youth Club




    年4月26日，“无界校园·青春共行”深圳大学城社团游园会在欢声笑语中圆满落幕。国际青年
  2026
  俱乐部（HSIYC）很荣幸代表哈工大深圳校区的国际群体参与其中。
  On April 26, 2026, the "Boundless Campus, Youth Together" Shenzhen University Town Club
  Carnival came to a joyful close. HITSZ International Youth Club (HSIYC) was honored to take part
  in the event representing the international community at HITSZ.




                                               00:54
当天现场气氛热烈，来自不同背景的同学和老师齐聚一堂，共同感受这场青春与文化交织的校园
盛会。
The venue was filled with energy, bringing together students and teachers from diverse backgrounds
for a celebration of youth and cultural exchange.




         展位互动 AT OUR BOOTH


   展位设置了许多别具特色的游戏和互动环节，例如抽取写有随机主题的折纸并将其快速画
HSIYC
出来，用棕榈叶托盘把小球按颜色分到两个玻璃罐中，以及更多精彩内容。
HSIYC booth hosted many unique games and activities such as picking a folded paper with a
random prompt and turning it into a quick drawing, using a palm-leaf tray to sort balls by color into
two jars, and many more.




大家都踊跃参与，现场始终充满欢笑、互动与惊喜，气氛十分热烈。
Participants joined in enthusiastically, and the booth stayed lively throughout the event, with plenty
of smiles, interaction, and surprise gifts.

与此同时，大家还积极在“万语留声”留言墙上写下自己母语中的问候语，让来自不同国家和文化
的声音在这里汇聚。
Meanwhile, many participants wrote greetings in their own languages on the "Voices of the World"
wall, bringing together warm messages from different cultural backgrounds.
        国际风采 WOLRD ON STAGE



除了展位互动，HSIYC带来的舞台表演也成为当天的一大亮点。来自不同国家的俱乐部成员同台
呈现，以整齐有力的舞步展现了国际青年的活力与风采。
In addition to the booth activities, the stage performance presented by HSIYC was another highlight
of the day. Club members from different countries took the stage together and showcased their
energy and style through coordinated dance steps.

现场掌声不断，观众的热情回应也让每一位表演者感受到了支持与鼓励。
The audience responded with continuous applause, giving every performer strong support and
encouragement.




        幸运抽奖 LUCKY DRAW



除了现场游戏与互动之外，幸运抽奖环节为展位增添了更多惊喜与期待。随着抽奖结果揭晓，获
奖者也收获了HSIYC精心准备的精美礼品。
In addition to the games and interactive activities, HSIYC also held a lucky draw, bringing even
more excitement and surprise to the booth. As the winners were announced, several participants
walked away with our unique gifts.
        文化交流 CULTURAL EXCHANGE



这次游园会不仅仅是一场社团展示，更是一场跨文化的交流与相遇。HSIYC展位迎来了来自不同
国家、不同背景的参与者，大家通过各项活动和表演共同分享欢乐、故事与联结，老师们也一同
加入其中，气氛十分热烈。
This carnival was more than a club showcase. It was a moment of intercultural exchange and
encounter. HSIYC booth welcomed participants from different countries and backgrounds, and
everyone shared joy, stories, and connections through the activities and performance, with teachers
also joining in the fun.
正是这样的开放与包容，让HSIYC的展位成为了一个真正意义上的“无界”空间。不同语言、不同
文化背景的交流在这里自然发生，欢笑、互动与参与也让整个展位始终保持着热烈而鲜活的氛
围。
It was this openness and inclusiveness that made our booth a truly “boundless” space. Different
languages and cultural backgrounds came together here naturally, while the laughter, interaction,
and enthusiastic participation kept the booth lively throughout the event.
感谢每一位来到HSIYC展位的朋友，感谢你们的支持、参与与笑容，也感谢所有为本次活动付出
努力的老师和工作人员。
Thank you to everyone who visited the HSIYC booth for your support, participation, and smiles,
and sincere thanks to all teachers and organizers who made this event possible.


未来，国际青年俱乐部将继续带来更多丰富有趣的活动，期待与大家在下一次相遇中继续创造属
于我们的精彩回忆。
In the future, HITSZ International Youth Club will continue to bring more exciting and meaningful
activities, and we look forward to creating more wonderful memories together at our next meeting.
