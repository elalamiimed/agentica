国际青年俱乐部 | 招募：「海岸课堂」深圳湾净滩志愿服务
HSIYC "Coast Classroom" Shenzhen Bay Beach Clean-Up

哈工大（深圳）国际青年俱乐部（HSIYC）「绿色校园 Green Campus」系列首场活动——「
海岸课堂」深圳湾净滩志愿服务，现面向全校同学正式招募。我们将在深圳湾公园日出剧场
至红树林段海岸线开展海岸清理与垃圾分类记录，用一次具体的行动，把环保理念真正落到
脚下。
HSIYC is now recruiting volunteers for "Coast Classroom," the first event of the club's Green
Campus series. We will carry out a coastal clean-up and waste-sorting survey along the Shenzhen
Bay Park coastline, from the Sunrise Theater to the mangrove section, turning environmental
ideas into concrete action.

01

活动概览

EVENT OVERVIEW

本次活动由哈工大（深圳）国际青年俱乐部（HSIYC）主办，国际教育中心与深圳市南山区义工
联合会合办。作为「绿色校园 Green Campus」系列的首场活动，我们希望以一次海岸清理，让
同学们把环保理念真正落到行动上。
The event is organised by HITSZ International Youth Club (HSIYC), with the International
Education Center and the Shenzhen Nanshan Volunteer Federation as co-organisers. As the first
event of the Green Campus series, it aims to turn environmental awareness into real action
through a single coastal clean-up.

活动时间 Time：2026年3月28日（周六）08:30-12:00 March 28, 2026 (Saturday), 08:30-12:00
集合地点 Meeting Point：哈工大（深圳）主楼 H 栋前广场 Plaza in front of Building H, Main
Building, HITSZ
活动地点 Venue：深圳湾公园日出剧场至红树林段海岸线 Shenzhen Bay Park coastline, from the
Sunrise Theater to the mangrove section
招募人数 Quota：限额 45 人（清理组 25 人、分类记录组 12 人、宣传组 8 人） 45 places in
total (Clean-Up Group 25, Sorting and Recording Group 12, Communications Group 8)

02

岗位设置

VOLUNTEER ROLES

Three groups are open for recruitment. Please choose the role that suits your interests and
physical condition, and indicate your preferred group in the registration form.

清理组（25 人） Clean-Up Group (25)：沿指定海岸线捡拾垃圾，配合小组长完成区域分工与安全提
醒。 Collect litter along the assigned coastline and follow the group leader's area
assignments and safety reminders.

分类记录组（12 人） Sorting and Recording Group (12)：对清理成果进行分类、称重与记录，汇
总本次净滩的数据。 Sort, weigh and record the collected waste, and compile the data from
this clean-up.

宣传组（8 人） Communications Group (8)：负责现场拍摄与文字记录，协助活动后的图文整理与传
播。 Handle on-site photography and written records, and support post-event
documentation and sharing.

03

物资与保障

SUPPLIES AND SUPPORT

手套、垃圾夹、垃圾袋由主办方统一提供；请自备水杯、防晒用品和一双防滑运动鞋。海边风大
，请勿穿凉鞋。
Gloves, litter pickers and rubbish bags are provided by the organisers. Please bring your own
water bottle, sun protection and a pair of non-slip sports shoes. The seaside is windy, so do
not wear sandals.

全程参与者可获得 4 小时志愿服务时长认证。如遇雨天活动顺延，具体安排另行通知。
Participants who stay for the whole session will receive a 4-hour volunteer service
certification. In case of rain, the event will be postponed and further arrangements will be
announced separately.

Closing

把理念落到海岸线上，把青春写进绿色校园。
Turn ideas into action on the coastline, and write youth into a green campus.

「海岸课堂」是俱乐部「绿色校园 Green Campus」系列的第一步，也是国际青年参与城市公共
事务的一次具体实践。我们期待与同学们在深圳湾相见，用一上午的时间，为这座城市的海岸线
留下一份干净与秩序。感谢国际教育中心与深圳市南山区义工联合会对本次活动的大力支持。
"Coast Classroom" is the first step of the club's Green Campus series and a concrete practice
of international youth participation in urban public affairs. We look forward to meeting you at
Shenzhen Bay and spending one morning bringing cleanliness and order to the city's coastline.
Special thanks to the International Education Center and the Shenzhen Nanshan Volunteer
Federation for their support.
