国际青年俱乐部 | 活动回顾：APEC智慧校园创新系列
Original   国际教育中心 哈工大深圳校区国际教育中心 Jul 21, 2026, 3:08 PM 广东
2026 年 11 月，深圳将迎来 APEC 峰会，哈工大（深圳）国际青年俱乐部（HSIYC）以此为
灵感，推出“APEC 智慧校园创新系列”——三场围绕亚太共同体核心理念的独立竞赛。
创新、合作 、开放，三大理念分别对应三场赛事，共同将我们的校园转化为 APEC 愿景的实
践缩影。如今三场赛事圆满落幕，让我们一同回顾这段旅程。

In November 2026, Shenzhen will host the APEC Summit. Inspired by this milestone, the
HITSZ International Youth Club (HSIYC) launched the APEC Smart Campus Innovation
Series — three independent competitions built around the core values of the Asia-Pacific
community.
Innovation, Cooperation, and Openness each value anchored one event, and together they
turned our campus into a living example of the APEC vision. Now that all three have come
to a close, let's look back on the journey.




                                  APEC | 未来技能挑战赛
                         01 Future Skills Challenge
                        Integration Bee Championship




首先开赛的是未来技能挑战赛——首届积分蜜蜂赛，该场赛事以“创新”为主题，在严格的时
间限制下，比拼准确度和速度。这是对微积分功底与临场心理素质的真正考验。

The first event — the inaugural HITSZ Integration Bee Championship — was themed
“Innovation". Under strict time limits, competing on accuracy and speed, it was a true test
of calculus proficiency and composure under pressure.




Preliminary Rounds
预赛
预赛采用快节奏的 1v1 赛制，两位选手同台对垒，实时解题。预赛阶段共有 32 名参赛者激
烈角逐，争夺决赛席位。经过一轮轮残酷的淘汰，最终仅有 4 位精英选手脱颖而出，挺进决
赛。

The preliminary rounds followed a blitz 1v1 format, where two contestants went
head‑to‑head solving integrals in real time. The preliminary round saw 32 competitors
battling fiercely for a spot in the finals. After a grueling series of eliminations, only 4 elite
finalists remained standing.

                     02 APEC | 游戏化系统设计大赛
                        Gamification Competition

                        Campus Gamification Design




 校园游戏化系统设计大赛为第二场竞赛，该场赛事以“合作”为主题，参赛者需运用积分、徽
 章、排行榜等游戏化机制，独立设计并演示一套解决真实校园问题的系统。这考验的不仅
 是创意，更是从想法到落地的执行力。

 The second event: the Campus Gamification Design Competition — was themed
 "Cooperation." Participants were challenged to independently design and demo a
gamified system addressing a real campus problem using mechanics like points, badges,
and leaderboards — where the real challenge lay not just in the idea, but in bringing it to
life.




Roadshow & Defense
路演与展示
最终共12位选手闯入决赛，带来了12套风格迥异的方案，涵盖学业打卡、健康习惯、校园社
交等多个方向，且每套系统都能现场实际运行。决赛采用"路演"形式，选手分设展位，评委
与其他参赛者可自由试玩系统、与设计者面对面交流，现场碰撞出不少灵感火花。路演之后
紧接着是正式的登台演讲环节，由多位教师评委组成评审团进行打分，为整场比赛增添了更
严谨的评审维度，全场洋溢着协作与活力的氛围，与本次比赛「合作」的主题相互呼应。


Twelve finalists brought 12 completely different systems to the table, from academic
tracking to wellness habits to campus social platforms, each running live on the day. The
finals took a roadshow format, with finalists at individual stations letting judges and peers
try each system firsthand and talk through the ideas behind it, sparking plenty of
spontaneous exchange. A round of formal presentations followed, where a panel of
teacher judges assessed each pitch, adding a sharper layer of scrutiny to the process — the
overall energy in the room matched the "Cooperation" spirit the competition was built
around.

                        03 APEC  | 校园想象画廊
                           Campus Imagination Galler y


                         Campus Ar twork Competition




最后一场竞赛为校园想象画廊，该场赛事以“开放”为主题，国际学生们运用AI工具或手绘，将
真实校园场景与APEC经济体文化元素相融合，创作出一批优秀作品。这是对想象力与跨文化表
达的一次生动展现。

The third event: the Campus Imagination Gallery — was themed "Openness." International
students used AI tools or hand-drawn techniques to create works fusing real HITSZ campus
scenes with cultural elements from APEC economies — a vivid showcase of imagination and
cross-cultural expression.
